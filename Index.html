Official Privacy Policy

Welcome to DEATH, a public multi-purpose Discord bot offering moderation, server security, automation, giveaways, utilities and entertainment features.

This policy explains what information DEATH processes, how it is used, where it may be stored and how users can submit privacy requests.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, DEATH may process:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames and display names.
  • Discord server, channel and role IDs.
  • Member join and leave events and role information.
  • Server configurations and bot settings.
  • Moderation records, including warnings, bans, kicks and timeouts.
  • Giveaway participation and related records.
  • Message content when necessary for commands, automoderation and security features.

2. Message Content Handling

DEATH processes message content only when necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Prefix commands and command arguments.
  • Spam detection and automatic moderation.
  • Keyword and Discord invitation filtering.
  • Deleted message logging.
  • Ticket transcript generation.
  • AFK-related responses.

Ordinary message content is not stored as persistent message-content records in the bot's SQLite database. However, some features may temporarily process or preserve message content.

  • Deleted messages may be cached temporarily in application memory, up to 10 messages per channel.
  • Configured logging features may send message content to designated Discord logging channels.
  • Ticket transcripts may be generated as HTML files containing conversation content and sent to configured Discord destinations.
  • AFK-related functionality may send relevant message information through Discord direct messages.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal information in messages or support tickets.

3. Database and Data Storage

DEATH currently uses SQLite as its database system.

Depending on enabled features, SQLite may store:

  • Discord user and server identifiers.
  • Message counts and leaderboard statistics.
  • Server configurations.
  • Moderation and security records.
  • Giveaway and feature-related information.

Message counts are stored for statistics and leaderboard functionality. This does not mean complete ordinary message contents are stored in the database.

Temporary message caches, generated ticket transcripts and Discord logging messages are handled separately from ordinary SQLite database records.

4. How We Use Information

  • Providing moderation and server management functionality.
  • Protecting servers against unauthorized destructive activities.
  • Detecting spam and configured rule violations.
  • Sending welcome messages and assigning automatic roles.
  • Managing giveaways and participation records.
  • Maintaining message counts and leaderboards.
  • Generating ticket transcripts and supporting AFK features.
  • Maintaining service reliability and security.

5. Data Sharing

DEATH does not sell user information or share it with unrelated third parties for advertising.

Information may be processed through Discord, hosting and infrastructure providers, database services and authorized operators responsible for maintaining DEATH.

Some information may be sent to Discord channels or users when server administrators enable moderation logs, ticket transcripts or AFK-related features.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by DEATH against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

These measures may include restricted database access, secure credential handling and administrative access controls. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

  • Database records may remain while required by their respective features.
  • Deleted message cache is temporary and limited to 10 messages per channel.
  • Moderation and security records may remain according to server configuration.
  • Ticket transcripts may remain in their configured Discord destinations or other configured storage until deleted.
  • Generated transcript files may also be temporarily handled by the bot's hosting environment.

We aim to delete information when it is no longer necessary, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

8. User Privacy Requests

Users and server administrators may contact the DEATH team to request information about eligible stored data, correction of inaccurate information or deletion of eligible information.

Requests may require reasonable verification to protect users against unauthorized access or deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate security requirements.

9. Server Administrator Controls

Server administrators can configure available DEATH functionality, including automoderation, moderation logging, welcome messages, autoroles, security protection, tickets and giveaways.

Disabling a feature does not automatically delete information previously generated by that feature. Separate deletion requests may be required.

10. AI and Machine Learning

DEATH does not use Discord message content or user information to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

11. Children's Privacy

DEATH operates within Discord and follows applicable age requirements and platform policies. We do not knowingly collect information in violation of applicable age-related laws or platform requirements.

12. Third-Party Services

DEATH relies on Discord and external infrastructure providers to operate.

Discord Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

This policy may be updated when DEATH's functionality, infrastructure or data-handling practices change. Updated versions will be published at this same location.
